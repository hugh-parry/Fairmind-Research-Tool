Research Tools

This is Fairmind's open-source research tool.

These tools gather market information — either from public listings, or from a supplier's own stock list you load in — to give a valuer a starting point for research. They do not produce valuations. Figures shown are asking prices, not adjusted for condition, provenance, completeness, or the purpose of the valuation. The appraisal itself follows separately.

Available tools

Watches

Watch market research

Enter a piece and get search links into Chrono24, Watchfinder, eBay and others, already filtered. Record what you find and the tool handles currency conversion, the median and mean, outlier flagging, and a dated research record you can export.

Open tool
Jewellery

Jewellery market research

Enter a piece and get search links into Sotheby's, Christie's, Bonhams and the retail market. Auction results and retail asking prices are recorded and summarised separately, with buyer's premium accounted for.

Open tool
Diamonds

Diamond stock list search

Load a supplier's loose diamond stock list (Gemex, Josyfon, Weissbart GIA) and search it against shape, weight, colour, clarity, lab and price criteria. Everything stays in your browser — nothing is uploaded.
