Research Tools

This is Fairmind's open-source research tool.

These tools gather publicly available market information to give a valuer a starting point for research. They do not produce valuations. Every figure shown is an asking price taken from a live listing on the day it was recorded, and is not adjusted for condition, provenance, completeness, or the purpose of the valuation. The appraisal itself follows separately.

Available tools

Watches

Watch market research

Enter a piece and get search links into Chrono24, Watchfinder, eBay and others, already filtered. Record what you find and the tool handles currency conversion, the median and mean, outlier flagging, and a dated research record you can export.

Open tool
Jewellery

Jewellery market research

Enter a piece and get search links into Sotheby's, Christie's, Bonhams and the retail market. Auction results and retail asking prices are recorded and summarised separately, with buyer's premium accounted for.
